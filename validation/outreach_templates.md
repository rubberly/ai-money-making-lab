# Outreach templates

Rules for every message:
- One person at a time.
- Use only the contact route they publish.
- Personalise the first line with something real.
- Include a free sample you actually made.
- Send one follow-up at most.
- Honour "no" immediately.

For email, include your real name, a physical postal address and a clear opt-out line (CAN-SPAM). Nothing in this project sends messages. You send them yourself.

---

## A. Content repurposing: first message (email or contact form)

**Subject:** Turned your "[episode title]" episode into a newsletter

> Hi [first name],
>
> I listened to [episode title]. The bit about [one specific point, with timestamp] stuck with me.
>
> I noticed you publish every week but [specific gap: there's no newsletter / LinkedIn has been quiet since March]. So I turned that episode into a newsletter issue and a LinkedIn post, written to sound like you, with every quote checked against the episode.
>
> Here it is: [link]. It's free and yours to use either way.
>
> If it's useful, I do this for every episode: newsletter, LinkedIn, an X thread and three clip picks with timestamps, back to you within 48 hours.
>
> Thanks for the show,
> [Your name]
> [Postal address] · Reply "no thanks" and I won't contact you again.

**LinkedIn DM version (short):**
> Hi [name], loved your episode on [topic], especially [point]. I turned it into a LinkedIn post in your voice as a free sample. Want me to send it over? No strings.

**Follow-up (once, 4–5 days later):**
> Hi [name], just making sure this didn't get buried: the free newsletter draft from [episode] is here: [link]. Happy to tweak it or leave it there. Either is fine.

**Reply to a positive response, offering the pilot:**
> Glad it helped! Easiest next step: a **one-episode pilot for $[150]**. You send the next episode, and within 48h you get the full pack (newsletter, LinkedIn, X thread, 3 clip picks, show notes, quotes). If you like it, the monthly plan is $[X] for 4 episodes. Want to try your next one?

## B. Website makeovers: first email

**Subject:** Your website on a phone ([business name])

> Hi [first name],
>
> I was looking for a [trade] in [town] and found you. Great reviews, by the way.
>
> On my phone, your site [specific problem #1, e.g. "shows the desktop layout, so the text is tiny"] and [problem #2, e.g. "the phone number isn't tappable"]. Most people searching for a [trade] are on a phone, so that can cost calls.
>
> I mocked up what a simple, mobile-friendly version could look like using your existing details: [screenshot or private preview link].
>
> If you'd like it, I can put it live on your domain for $[300–400], and keep it updated for $[30]/month if you want. If not, no problem. Feel free to pass the audit to whoever runs your site.
>
> [Your name]
> [Postal address] · Reply "stop" and I won't email again.

**Follow-up (once):**
> Hi [name], quick follow-up on the mobile mock-up for [business]. Happy to fix just the tap-to-call button for $[100] if a full refresh isn't right now.

## Customer-research questions (5–10 minute calls, both offers)

Ask about the past, not the future. "Would you buy?" is worthless. "What did you do last time?" is useful.

1. How do you handle [newsletter / website updates] today? Who does it?
2. When did you last try to improve it? What happened?
3. Have you paid anyone or any tool for this before? How much? Why did it stop?
4. What would have to be true for this to be worth $[price] a month to you?
5. What's the worst thing someone could do with your content / your website?
6. Who else do you know with the same problem?

Write answers into the prospect's notes in the app the same day.
