# Which opportunities to prototype, and why

I selected **two**, not three. Content repurposing is clearly the strongest. Website makeovers is a distinct second, and software genuinely changes how well it works. Chatbots (#3) and listing makeovers (#4) don't need custom software: the SaaS tools already *are* the product. So building for them would have been building for its own sake.

Neither choice depends on paid ads, a large audience, or a paid subscription to start.

---

## Opportunity A: "Podcast to Posts", a done-for-you content-repurposing service (primary)

| | |
|---|---|
| **1. Target customer** | Solo creators and small-business owners who publish a **weekly podcast or YouTube show** (roughly 1k–50k audience) and have **one obvious missing channel**: no newsletter, a dead LinkedIn, or no clips. Best sub-niches are ones where the creator *sells something*: coaches, consultants, B2B founders, local experts. For them, more reach means money, so they'll pay. |
| **2. Problem** | Their best ideas reach only the people who already listen. Repurposing takes 3–5 hours per episode. DIY AI tools give generic, "robotic" output that can misquote them. |
| **3. Product** | Within 48h of each episode: a newsletter, a LinkedIn post, an X thread, a 3-clip plan with timestamps, show notes and a quote bank. All in their voice, every fact checked against the episode, approved by them. |
| **4. Revenue** | Free sample → **$150 one-episode pilot** → **$400–600/mo for 4 episodes** (starting prices; estimates). Upsell: posting/scheduling for them. |
| **5. Differentiation** | Against **DIY tools** (Castmagic ~$39/mo): voice-matched, human-edited, fact-checked, zero effort for them. Against **"paste into ChatGPT" freelancers**: a systematic QC trail. Against **agencies** ($350–1,200+/mo): cheaper and personal. |
| **6. Smallest useful version** | Built: **Repurpose Desk**. Voice profile → transcript → drafts → automatic QC → approve → client pack, plus prospect pipeline, time and revenue tracking, and an offer page. |
| **7. Validate demand** | 15 personal free samples to hand-picked creators in one niche. Measure replies, then paid pilots. No ads. |
| **8. Key assumptions to test** | (a) Creators reply to a free sample from a stranger (target ≥20% reply rate). (b) Some pay $100–150 for a pilot (target ≥1 of 15). (c) Delivery takes ≤2h per episode by episode 3, so the hourly rate is ≥$40. (d) They keep paying monthly (retention after month 2). |

## Opportunity B: "Site Makeover", audit-led website makeovers for local trades (secondary)

| | |
|---|---|
| **1. Target customer** | Local service businesses (gardeners, plumbers, cleaners, dog groomers) whose site fails on mobile, has no click-to-call, or looks abandoned, **and** who show signs of caring about getting customers online (active Google Business profile, recent reviews, running ads). |
| **2. Problem** | They lose calls from mobile visitors. A redesign seems expensive ($1,000–5,000+ from freelancers) and slow. |
| **3. Product** | A free, specific audit ("your phone number isn't tappable; the site has no mobile layout") plus a spec one-page site built from their real details. Then launch on their domain and an optional care plan. |
| **4. Revenue** | **$300–600 launch fee** (one page, their domain) + **$25–50/mo care plan** for hosting, edits and checks (starting prices; estimates). |
| **5. Differentiation** | An evidence-first pitch (a real audit of *their* site, not "I make websites"). Focus on calls and bookings, not looks. Priced well below freelance market rates. |
| **6. Smallest useful version** | Built: **Site Makeover Kit**. URL audit with SSRF-safe fetching → client-friendly report → spec site generator → outreach draft with CAN-SPAM footer → pipeline → offer page. |
| **7. Validate demand** | Audit 30 sites in one trade and town. Build 5 spec sites. Send 10 personal emails. Measure replies, calls and sales. |
| **8. Key assumptions** | (a) An audit-led email gets ≥10% replies. (b) ≥1 of 20 buys at $300+. (c) A spec site takes ≤90 minutes. (d) ≥1 of 3 buyers takes the care plan. |

## Why not the others (for now)

- **Support chatbots**: a decent third option. Test it later as an **add-on** to website clients ("want a chatbot that answers your FAQs?"). It needs no app.
- **Listing makeovers**: workable only as an honest photo refresh with disclosure. Margins are squeezed by $1.60/image services.
- **Dead-lead reactivation**: postpone. Pay-to-play software plus consent law is a bad first business.
- **AI UGC ads**: postpone. Learn ad strategy first, and never script fake first-person testimonials.
- **Method 7**: unknown. Evaluate it with this scorecard if you attend the free event.
