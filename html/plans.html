7-day validation plans

Goal: learn whether real people will pay, not just whether they say "nice". Keep interest and payment separate in every count. Spend: $0. No ads, no bulk messaging, no bought lists.

Plan A: Content repurposing (Repurpose Desk)

Who to approach. Creators with a weekly podcast or YouTube show, 10+ episodes, an audience of roughly 1k–50k, who sell something (coaching, consulting, a product, a B2B service), and who are missing one channel: no newsletter, LinkedIn quiet for 3+ months, or no Shorts/Reels.

Where to find them. Apple Podcasts and Spotify category charts in one niche (e.g. "Business → Entrepreneurship"); YouTube search for niche + "podcast" filtered to the last month; Listen Notes search; LinkedIn posts that link to podcast episodes. Use only the contact route they publish (website contact form, listed business email, LinkedIn DM).

Day Actions Output
1 Pick one niche. Fill in your settings in Repurpose Desk (name, prices). Find 20 prospects and log each with the specific gap you saw. 20 prospects in the pipeline
2 Choose the 5 best fits. For each, get a recent episode transcript (YouTube captions or Whisper) and build a voice profile from their own posts. 5 episodes created
3 Produce the newsletter + LinkedIn post for 3 of them. Clear every QC flag. Read each one aloud. Would they say it? 3 approved samples
4 Send 3 personal messages (template in outreach_templates.md) with the sample attached or linked. Set stage = "Free sample sent". 3 sent
5 Make and send samples for the other 2. Start the next 5. 5 sent total
6 Reply to every response within hours. To anyone positive: offer the $150 pilot (or $100 if they hesitate) for one full episode pack. Pilot offers
7 One polite follow-up to non-responders, then stop. Log all metrics in experiment_tracker.csv. Decide using the rules below. Decision

Milestones (week 1 → week 3)

  • Relevant creators contacted with a sample: 5 → 15
  • Replies (any): ≥1 → ≥3 (interest)
  • Asked for more or a call: ≥1 → ≥2 (stronger interest)
  • Paid pilots: ≥1 by end of week 3 (payment)
  • Delivery time per episode: record it, target ≤2h by your third episode

Decision rules (after 15 samples)

  • Continue: ≥1 paid pilot, or ≥3 replies asking about price. Then raise the price for the next 5.
  • Change: replies but no payment → change niche (choose creators who sell something) or offer (cheaper pilot, a different deliverable mix). 0 replies → your samples or targeting are off. Have someone honest critique 2 samples, then re-target.
  • Stop: 30 samples, 0 paid pilots, after one niche change.

Plan B: Website makeovers (Site Makeover Kit)

Who to approach. One trade in one town or region (e.g. "gardeners in [your county]"). Qualify for: a website that exists but is bad (no mobile layout, no click-to-call, outdated), plus signs they want work (Google Business profile with recent reviews, active social posts, or Google Ads).

Where to find them. Google Maps search [trade] in [town]. Click each website. Run the Site Makeover Kit audit.

Day Actions Output
1 Audit 30 sites in the kit. Log each as a prospect with its score. 30 audits
2 Pick the 5 worst sites with the best businesses (good reviews, bad site). Build spec sites from their real details. 5 spec sites
3 Check every detail (hours, services, phone). Use only reviews they've published publicly, with attribution, or leave them out. 5 checked sites
4 Send 5 personal emails with the audit's top 2 issues, a screenshot, and the CAN-SPAM footer. Don't host spec sites publicly or let them be indexed. Send screenshots or a password-protected preview. 5 sent
5 Audit 10 more. Build 3 more spec sites. Send them. 8 sent total
6 Call back anyone who replied. Offer the $300–400 launch or a $100 quick fix (click-to-call, mobile tweaks). Offers
7 One polite follow-up. Log metrics. Decide. Decision

Milestones

  • Audits: 30 → 60
  • Emails sent: 8 → 20
  • Replies: ≥1 → ≥2 (interest)
  • Sales: ≥1 by week 3 (payment), even if it's the $100 quick fix
  • Spec site build time: target ≤90 min

Decision rules (after 20 emails)

  • Continue: ≥1 paid job or ≥2 calls. Add a care plan to every sale.
  • Change: replies but no sale → try a different trade, or lead with the $100 quick fix. No replies → shorten the email and lead with one concrete problem plus a screenshot.
  • Stop: 40 emails, 0 sales.

Measuring demand

Interest (encouraging, not proof) Payment (proof)
Replies, "looks great", calls booked, offer-page form fills, test-mode "Reserve" clicks Money received (pilot, launch fee, first monthly payment), a second payment from the same client

Log only real payments in the app's Payments section. The dashboard keeps the two columns separate on purpose.
