DuPage plan: Website makeovers + Quotehound

One kind of customer, two products. Local tradespeople in DuPage County (plumbers, roofers, landscapers, painters…) need two things: to get found on their phone (a website that works) and to win the quotes they send (follow-up). You sell the first with the Site Makeover Kit and the second with Quotehound.

  • Website makeover = fast cash and trust. One sale is worth $350, and an owner can say yes in one call.
  • Quotehound = income that repeats. $29 every month, with no extra work from you per customer.
  • Together, the website gets you in the door, and Quotehound keeps paying you after.

All prices are starting points and every result below is an estimate, not a promise.

Step 0: Do these before contacting anyone (about 1–2 hours)

# Task Why
1 Connect Stripe to Quotehound and add the payment links (monthly $29, annual $290). Right now Quotehound can’t take money. Nothing else matters until this works.
2 Make payment links in your own Stripe or PayPal for website work: $100 quick fix, $350 makeover, $175 deposit. So a "yes" can turn into money on the same call.
3 Check the name "Quotehound" at tmsearch.uspto.gov. An insurance company already uses "QuoteHound". Check before you print anything.
4 Add your name and mailing address in Site Makeover Kit → Settings. US email law (CAN-SPAM) requires it on sales emails.
5 Do both practice rounds: ★ Start here in each app. So your first real demo isn’t your first try.
6 Open Quotehound on your phone and send yourself a test quote. You’ll demo it to trades on their phones.

The offer ladder

What one bundle customer is worth (estimate): $350 once + $59/month, or about $1,060 in the first year.

Sort the 200-business list into 3 groups

The list has each business’s trade, town, public phone, website, email (when published) and a note.

Group How to spot them Lead with
A: no website notes say "no website found" Makeover ($350). Easiest win: "you don’t show up properly on phones".
B: bad website Site Makeover Kit audit score under 55 Free audit + mock-up, then the makeover
C: good website Audit score 55+ Quotehound trial first: "Your site’s fine. What happens after you send a quote?"

Run the audit on each website (Site Makeover Kit → Audit). Copy the score into your list.

The 4-week plan

Week 1: sort and prepare

  • Day 1: Do Step 0. Pick one trade to start (roofers or landscapers: they quote a lot and look at websites on their phone).
  • Day 2: Audit the websites of 30 businesses in that trade. Mark each A / B / C.
  • Day 3: Build 5 sample sites for the best A/B businesses (good reviews, bad or no site).
  • Day 4: Send 10 emails (scripts below): 5 makeover emails to A/B, 5 Quotehound emails to C.
  • Day 5: Call 10 businesses from the list. Ask for the owner, use the phone script. Manual calls only.

Week 2: follow up and demo

  • One short follow-up to everyone from week 1 who hasn’t replied. Then stop.
  • Another 20 contacts (10 email, 10 calls), widening to a second trade.
  • Every conversation: show, don’t tell. Show the mock-up on your phone, or send a Quotehound test quote to their phone.
  • Goal: 2 calls or visits booked.

Week 3: close and deliver

  • Offer one specific next step with a price: "I can put it live this week for $350. Want me to?"
  • Take the deposit or full payment before you start.
  • Deliver within 3 days. Start their Quotehound trial the day the site goes live, and add a "Request a quote" button that goes to it.
  • Ask every customer for one review and one referral ("Who else do you know who quotes jobs?").

Week 4: count and decide

Log real money only. Then use the rules at the bottom.

Phone opener (manual calls only)

"Hi, is this the owner? My name’s [name]. I’m local in [town] and I help [trade]s get more calls from their website and win more of the quotes they send. Got 30 seconds? … On my phone your website [problem]. I mocked up a fix. Can I text or email you a screenshot?"

If they ask for a text, send one message, since they asked. Don’t start texting businesses who haven’t asked.

The bundle pitch (after a makeover sale)

"Your new site has a ‘Request a quote’ button. Want the quotes it brings in followed up automatically too? Care plan plus Quotehound is $59 a month together. The first 14 days of Quotehound are free."

Common objections

They say You say
"My nephew did my site." "That’s great. One thing I noticed: [one problem]. Easy fix if you ever want it." Then stop.
"I get all my work from referrals." "Makes sense. Referrals still look you up on their phone before they call. Quotehound helps you win the quotes those referrals ask for."
"I already use Jobber / Housecall Pro." "Then you’re set for scheduling. Quotehound only does quote follow-up, and it’s $29. Want to try it free on your next few quotes?"
"Too expensive." Offer the $100 quick fix or the free Quotehound trial. Keep the door open.

Rules that keep you safe

  • Emails: real name, mailing address, a working "stop" line, and stop forever when someone opts out (CAN-SPAM).
  • Calls: dial by hand, never use robocalls or auto-dialers, and stop if someone asks.
  • Texts: only to people who asked you to text them. Automatic texts in Quotehound go to their customers, so the trade must have their customers’ permission.
  • Honesty: no made-up reviews, numbers or "clients like you got 3× more calls". Show the real mock-up and the real app instead.
  • The lead list stays private. It is in private/, which is never uploaded to GitHub.

Keep going, change or stop (after 4 weeks)

  • Keep going: at least 1 paid makeover or 2 paying Quotehound subscribers. Raise the makeover price to $450 and add a second trade.
  • Change: people reply but don’t pay. Lead with the $100 quick fix or the free Quotehound trial, or switch trade.
  • Stop or rethink Quotehound: 30+ real conversations and nobody keeps it past the trial. Ask those people why, and write the answers down. That’s the most valuable thing you’ll learn.
