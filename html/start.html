Start here

You don't need to read everything. Follow this page from top to bottom.

The short version

  1. The video shows seven ways to make money with AI. Only six are actually explained. All six are services where you use AI tools to do work for a business.
  2. The research scored all seven for a beginner. The best start is content repurposing: turning a creator's podcast or video into newsletters and posts. Website makeovers for local businesses come second.
  3. Two apps were built to help you do this work. Each has a ★ Start here course inside it that walks you through it step by step.

Your path (pick one app and stick with it for 2 weeks)

Not sure? Pick Repurpose Desk. It is cheaper to start and scored highest.

In the app, click ★ Start here and do the lessons in order:

  1. Learn how the business works (5 min)
  2. Do the practice round with sample data (10 min)
  3. Pick one niche (15–20 min)
  4. Find 20–30 prospects (45–60 min)
  5. Make your first real free samples (about 1 hour)
  6. Send them, one person at a time (10–15 min each)
  7. Turn a "yes" into a paid pilot, then deliver and decide

Every app page also has a "How to use this page" box at the top.

Three rules that matter most

  • Interest isn't money. Replies and "looks great!" feel good, but only a payment proves the business works.
  • Never make things up. No invented numbers, quotes or reviews. The apps check for this, and so should you.
  • One person at a time. Write personal messages, use only contact details people publish themselves, follow up once, and always accept a "no".

When to keep going, change or stop

  • Keep going if you get at least 1 paid pilot from your first 15–20 samples. Then raise your price.
  • Change your niche or offer if people reply but nobody pays.
  • Stop after 30–40 samples with no sales (and one change), and try the other app instead.
